Controls

Oxygen (O₂)
5.0%
Most stem cells prefer low oxygen (about 2–8%) called “physiologic hypoxia.” Too low = stress/no division; too high = oxidative stress.
Growth factors dose
45%
Growth factors are protein “instructions.” Too little = cells idle; too much (especially in older cultures) = pushes risky overgrowth.
Passage number
P5
Passage ≈ how many times we’ve split the culture. Higher passage = “older” cells → closer to Hayflick’s limit and instability.
Tip 2–8% O₂ · moderate GF · early passage

Outcome

Controlled self‑renewal / poised to differentiate
O₂ within physiologic hypoxia, moderate growth signals, and early passage support healthy stem‑cell behavior.
